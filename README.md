## About Me

I focus on defensive security and the practical side of cyber defense. My interest is in how organizations monitor their environments, detect suspicious activity, investigate it properly, and improve controls so threats are reduced before they turn into damage.

## What Blue Team means to me

Blue Team is the defensive side of security: the people who protect an organization, reduce exposure, and respond when something goes wrong.

For me, that means five core responsibilities:

- Monitor: collect and review logs from endpoints, servers, and networks
- Detect: create and tune rules that identify real threats without overwhelming analysts
- Respond: triage alerts, investigate, contain threats, and document findings
- Harden: reduce the attack surface through patching, MFA, least privilege, and secure configurations
- Learn: turn every incident into a better detection, a clearer playbook, or a stronger control
